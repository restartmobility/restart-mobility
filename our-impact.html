Real equipment. Real journey. Real impact.

From donation to dignity

Our first medical-bed journey shows the practical model ReStart Mobility is building: useful equipment donated in the UK, transported to Romania, carefully installed and put into use by a person who needed it.

1. Donated & collected in the UK

A donated electric medical bed was collected and prepared for its journey rather than being left unused.

2. Transported to Romania

Transport connected a UK donation with a person who needed practical equipment in Romania.

3. Assembled & installed

The bed was carefully assembled and prepared for safe everyday use in the beneficiary's home.

Before

Everyday positioning, comfort and care were more difficult without suitable equipment.

After

The donated electric medical bed is now in use, helping provide safer positioning, greater comfort and better support for daily care.
