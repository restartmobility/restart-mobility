Donate mobility equipment in the UK

If you have mobility or care equipment that is no longer needed, ReStart Mobility CIC would be grateful to hear from you. Useful equipment can be collected, prepared, refurbished where appropriate and put back into use.

Offer equipment by email WhatsApp us

Equipment we may be able to accept

♿ Manual and electric wheelchairs
🛵 Mobility scooters and knee scooters
🏥 Patient hoists and transfer equipment
🛏️ Electric and medical beds
🪜 Ramps and access equipment
🦯 Walking and assistive equipment

Why donate to ReStart Mobility?

Reduce waste

Equipment that is still safe and useful should not be discarded simply because it is no longer needed by its original owner.

Create practical impact

Donated mobility equipment can improve comfort, safer care and independence for someone who genuinely needs it.

Support a social mission

ReStart Mobility is building a sustainable model that supports people in the UK and Romania.

How donation works

1. Tell us what equipment you have and where you are located.
2. Send a few clear photos and, if known, the make/model.
3. We confirm whether the equipment is suitable.
4. We agree practical collection or delivery arrangements.

What information helps us most?

Please include your postcode area, equipment type, condition, dimensions if relevant, and whether it can be dismantled for transport.

Acceptance depends on condition, safety, storage and transport capacity. We cannot guarantee collection of every item.

Have equipment you no longer need?

Send us the details. A wheelchair, scooter, hoist or medical bed sitting unused could become valuable support for someone else.
