What ReStart Mobility can help with

Mobility equipment

Wheelchairs, mobility aids and other equipment that may improve everyday independence.

Care equipment

Medical beds, hoists and transfer equipment where suitable items and resources are available.

Affordable options

Selected refurbished equipment may be offered in the UK at prices below typical market rates.

Important

ReStart Mobility is not an emergency medical service and cannot guarantee that every request can be fulfilled. Availability depends on donated stock, suitability, safety, transport, funding and the individual circumstances of the request.

If a person has an urgent medical or safeguarding need, please use the appropriate health or emergency service.
